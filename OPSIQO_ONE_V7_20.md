# OPSIQO ONE V7.20 — Stabilization, Translation & Connected Knowledge

V7.20 is a completion-oriented release. It does not introduce broad new AI authority. It reduces high-value localization gaps, expands authenticated accessibility coverage, and connects explicit program/funding evidence into the permission-scoped Knowledge Graph and Scenario Lab.

## Implemented

- Reviewed EN/FR/ES/AR exact-string coverage for Recruiting, Performance and Compensation.
- Shared legacy surface translation runtime remains evidence-preserving and Arabic-RTL compatible.
- Translation inventory: 455 exact reviewed candidates across 9 catalogued surfaces; 3,325 heuristic candidates remain.
- Authenticated browser accessibility matrix adds Performance and Compensation and requires Arabic reviewed markers for Recruiting, Performance and Compensation.
- Knowledge Graph adds explicit `project`, `funding_source`, `project_funding`, `worker_project`, `worker_funding`, and `project_org_unit` relationships when `workforce.read` is available.
- Scenario Lab/Digital Twin and Intelligence Hub expose project and funding node counts.
- Safe Execute remains frozen to `notifications.mark_visible_read` only.

## Governance boundaries

- Project/funding graph relationships come only from explicit OPSIQO project, funding and allocation records.
- No compensation, health, case or private-contact data is added to the graph.
- The graph does not infer donor/funding eligibility, legal obligations or employment outcomes.
- Reviewed translation coverage is not a claim that all legacy screens are linguistically complete.
- Browser accessibility tooling remains implementation/UAT evidence, not a WCAG 2.2 AA conformance certificate.
- No new AI Execute capability is authorized in V7.20.
