## Evidence-vault integrity controls
- Evidence sources are restricted to approved internal source collections or HTTPS external references.
- Governed source SHA-256 values are inherited/validated where available.
- Evidence immutable metadata is hashed with SHA-256.
- A genesis ledger entry is created with each evidence record.
- Governed evidence lifecycle changes append ledger entries.
- Ledger verification checks sequence, previous-hash linkage and entry hashes.
- Metadata/chain mismatch quarantines evidence instead of silently rewriting history.
- Automated integrity mismatch handling now creates audit evidence as well as notification state.
- Legal hold blocks retention expiry until independently released.

## Assurance planning / testing controls
- Assurance plan approval requires independent `assurance.approve` authority.
- Applicable control tests cannot be submitted without evidence references.
- Test approval is segregated from creator/submitting tester.
- Partial/ineffective approved tests require a documented finding before plan completion.
- Open findings block plan closure unless resolved or formally accepted through the governed path.
- Finding risk acceptance, validation and closure are approval-gated.
- CAPA approval and verification are independently governed; verified CAPA can then be formally closed.
- Executive/Board assurance report approval is independently governed.
