# OPSIQO H51.34 — Record-Bound Education Provenance

Parent H51.33 SHA: `6f3357d5b555e2fb73e1369c38b3b12648ec5922`.

H51.34 disables section-wide propagation of record-specific education dates.
Each degree/institution record receives date/status/location evidence only from
its own bounded education block. Foreign dates and reused expected-completion
evidence become critical readiness issues.

H51.33 certification completeness, H51.32 semantic purity and Current safeguards,
transactional repair, final candidateVerificationGate, and original Fit evidence
authority are preserved. Production remains untouched during certification.
