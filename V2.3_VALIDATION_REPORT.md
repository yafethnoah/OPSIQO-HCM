## v2.3 governance hardening verified in source
- HR Partner receives `governance.read`, `governance.manage`, `governance.audit` but not `governance.approve`.
- Governance control approval is blocked for the creator or submitter.
- Attestation approval is blocked for the submitter; submission is limited to the assigned role or a governance approver.
- Exception approval is blocked for the creator or submitter.
- Accepted risk and risk closure require governance approval and independent review.
- Diagnostic controls and active policies can bootstrap into the enterprise control register without changing their source records.
- Bootstrap uses deterministic IDs and chunks writes to avoid Firestore batch-limit overflow.
- Source review, overdue attestation and exception-expiry notifications use dedupe records.
- Governance control status explicitly remains an internal evidence/governance state, not a legal compliance conclusion.
- A pre-existing duplicate AI Firestore test incorrectly declared inside `beforeAll` was removed; the proper top-level AI rules test remains.
