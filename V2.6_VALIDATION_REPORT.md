## Privacy governance boundaries
- Processing activities retain qualified human authority/lawful-basis review and `legalConclusionProhibited` semantics.
- The Privacy Center does not infer which privacy statute applies to an organization or employee relationship.
- Privacy-request statutory due dates are user/qualified-review inputs; the workflow does not calculate one universal legal deadline.
- Privacy incident notification decisions begin `pending_human_review` and require independent `privacy.approve` authority plus case-specific rationale.
- Request fulfillment, denial, incident notification decision, root-cause and remediation actions require case-specific user-entered evidence/rationale rather than pre-filled legal conclusions.
- Cross-border transfer approval requires an approved transfer/privacy impact assessment; transfer legality/sufficiency remains a qualified human determination.
- Consequential individual-employment AI use cannot be approved through the Privacy Center.
- Automated/high-risk personal-data AI use requires an approved AIA/PIA/DPIA before approval.
- Privacy and AI readiness scoring is explicitly operational and non-certifying.

## Reference-design posture
The v2.6 reference pack intentionally distinguishes legal applicability from governance design. Federal PIPEDA employment coverage is not assumed for every Ontario employer; federal breach/AIA/PIA material and NIST AI RMF are used as governance references only where appropriate. Organization-specific applicability and legal conclusions remain qualified human decisions.
