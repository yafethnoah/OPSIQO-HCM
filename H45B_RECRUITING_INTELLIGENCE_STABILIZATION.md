# OPSIQO H45B — Recruiting Intelligence Stabilization

H45B closes the live UAT interview-selection defect and upgrades deterministic/hybrid resume parsing plus ATS evidence matching without changing OPSIQO's human-decision boundary.

## Interview workflow
- Newly scheduled interviews are selected automatically after the authoritative POST succeeds and the interview list refreshes.
- The generated interview kit is loaded immediately instead of leaving the prior interview selected.
- Legacy/missing kits are explicitly identified and can be generated by a recruiting manager through the governed kit endpoint.
- Kit generation remains draft → human review → lock; no interview score can be submitted before lock.
- Requisition question-bank core questions are still reused for comparability and are published only after a human locks the interview kit.

## Resume parsing v3
- Better header/name extraction with filename fallback preserved.
- More robust location, headline, summary, skills, education, certifications, job titles and employer extraction.
- Supports additional experience headings such as Career History, Work History and Employment Experience.
- Employment duration is calculated only from a reliably isolated employment section. Education/project dates cannot be silently counted as employment tenure.
- Supports month-name, bare-year, MM/YYYY and YYYY-MM employment ranges and merges overlapping periods.
- Governed-AI extraction is merged with deterministic evidence rather than replacing it. AI-reported tenure is not trusted unless dated employment evidence can be deterministically verified from the extracted resume text.
- Parse-quality, extraction signals, titles, employers, skills and warnings are displayed to the recruiter before consent. Parse quality is explicitly labelled as extraction quality—not candidate quality.
- Candidate data remains transient until recruiter review and recorded consent.

## ATS Evidence Engine v3
- Semantic alias groups and light stemming improve equivalent job-language matching while preserving deterministic reproducibility.
- Requirement matching records match score, method, matched concepts/terms and evidence snippets.
- Multi-concept requirements are conjunctive when written with `and`: proving only Google Ads does not satisfy `Google Ads and GA4`. `or`/`either` alternatives remain alternatives.
- Requirements-section parsing accepts short explicit requirements such as `CRM` and stops cleanly at the next JD section.
- Experience evidence uses normalized dated-employment ranges and does not infer duration from education history.
- Job-related semantic signals replace raw exact-token-only comparisons in skill/responsibility evidence.
- Protected/sensitive criteria remain excluded from automated scoring.
- Score remains an internal evidence-alignment indicator, not a prediction of any third-party ATS and never drives automatic rejection, advancement or hiring.

## UAT focus

2. Verify education dates do not inflate experience tenure.
3. Run ATS review and inspect semantic match methods and missing concepts.
4. Schedule a new interview and confirm the newly created interview is selected automatically with its draft kit displayed.
5. Open a legacy interview without a kit and confirm the governed Generate structured interview kit recovery action appears.
6. Lock a reviewed kit, submit interviewer evidence, and confirm no AI action changes candidate disposition automatically.
