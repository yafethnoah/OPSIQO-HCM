## Supply-chain / resilience limitations
- The OPSIQO SBOM generator is lockfile-inventory evidence; it is not represented as exhaustive runtime/component discovery.
- The provenance generator deliberately does not claim a SLSA level and does not locally sign provenance.
- The bounded deterministic source scan is not a replacement for dedicated SAST/secret-scanning tooling.
- OPSIQO does not directly enable or verify Firebase/cloud PITR or backup services; provider-side capability requires independent cloud evidence.
- Platform readiness does not certify secure development, SLSA level, SBOM completeness, disaster recovery, compliance or availability.
- Platform/security evidence cannot independently create an employment decision or disciplinary outcome.
