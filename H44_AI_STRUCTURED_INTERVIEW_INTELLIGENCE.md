# OPSIQO H44 — AI Structured Interview Intelligence

H44 upgrades Recruiting from interview scheduling plus a fixed three-criterion scorecard into a governed structured-interview system.

## Delivered
- automatic interview-kit generation when an interview is scheduled;
- standardized core questions derived from reviewed requisition requirements;
- behavioral, situational and technical questions;
- candidate-specific verification probes derived from ATS evidence gaps;
- follow-up probes and expected-evidence guidance;
- anchored 1/3/5 rating standards;
- draft → review → lock interview-kit lifecycle;
- locked-kit requirement before immutable scorecard submission;
- reusable requisition question bank so locked core questions remain comparable across candidates;
- optional governed-AI question refinement with deterministic fallback;
- protected/sensitive-trait filters and explicit no-decision boundary;
- per-interviewer immutable scorecards;
- panel aggregate statistics and high-variance warning;
- audit records for kit creation, regeneration, locking and scorecards;
- no automatic hiring, rejection, advancement or protected-characteristic inference.

## UAT flow
1. Schedule an interview for an eligible candidate.
2. Select the interview in **AI structured interview intelligence**.
3. Review generated standardized and candidate-specific questions.
4. Regenerate while still draft if needed.
5. Lock the interview kit.
6. Conduct the interview and record evidence/rating for each scored question.
7. Submit the immutable interviewer scorecard.
8. For panels, repeat from each assigned interviewer and review panel consistency / variance.
