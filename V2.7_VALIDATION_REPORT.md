## Workforce resilience governance verified
- Critical-role resilience uses role/process coverage rather than employee ranking.
- Single-point-of-failure roles with insufficient coverage/no ready successor escalate deterministically to Critical risk.
- Workforce BIA captures maximum tolerable downtime (MTD), recovery time objective (RTO), minimum staffing, dependencies and 1–5 operational impact dimensions.
- RTO greater than MTD is rejected by the governed service.
- Continuity plans require human-defined activation criteria, minimum staffing, alternate-work arrangements, communications and recovery objectives.
- Crisis command records incidents, staffing gaps and communications without automatically selecting individual employees for deployment.
- Recovery plans cannot complete until required milestones are complete; independent verification is required before closure.
- Exercise review is independently governed and records target versus actual recovery performance, lessons and actions.
- Executive/Board resilience reports remain point-in-time operational assurance records rather than legal compliance or certification outputs.
- AI Copilot integration receives aggregate resilience/readiness evidence only when the actor has `resilience.read`; no individual succession/deployment ranking is exposed by the resilience evidence adapter.

## RBAC / segregation of duties
- Added `resilience.read`, `resilience.manage`, `resilience.approve`, `resilience.audit`, and `resilience.incident`.
- HR Partner can read/manage/audit/operate incidents but does **not** receive `resilience.approve`.
- Managers can receive limited resilience read/incident capability but cannot manage or approve resilience governance records.
- Employees do not receive Resilience Center access by default.
- Independent approval/closure/review boundaries are enforced for governed resilience actions.
